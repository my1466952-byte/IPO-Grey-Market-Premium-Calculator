## What Is an IPO Grey Market Premium?

Grey Market Premium (GMP) refers to the additional amount at which IPO shares are traded in the unofficial market before their stock exchange listing. A positive GMP may indicate strong market interest, whereas a negative GMP can suggest weaker sentiment. However, GMP is unofficial and does not guarantee actual listing performance.

## How Does an IPO GMP Calculator Work?

An IPO GMP Calculator uses two important values: the IPO issue price and the current GMP. After entering these details, investors can calculate the estimated listing price and potential listing gain percentage.

For example, if an IPO issue price is ₹500 and its GMP is ₹100, the estimated listing price becomes ₹600. The potential listing gain is approximately 20%.

**Listing Gain (%) = (GMP ÷ Issue Price) × 100**
